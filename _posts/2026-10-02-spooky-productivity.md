The tech overlords have signed a demonic blood contract and now we are held up to the fire. All this talk about productivity is unsettling when you realize how it manifest in your job. Everyone I know is working way harder than before AI. Demanding deadlines seem never ending and most large companies seem to to drinking the token maxing kool-aid. The sad part is that there is some truth to this "boost" in productivity but this boost is being misapplied by many. With Halloween coming up lets's explore the spooky side of this AI or die life we live now.

## Skeletons in the Closet: Death by Merge Request

The current siren call is that using AI can boost productivity which means you can ship faster and more features. The issues now are the hundreds of MRs devs have to "review". Now since there are so many MRs we have people pushing with no code review or they are using multi agent code reviews which is a fancy way of saying we don't know how to read or be responsible.

This is all done because people have lost their minds and their managers or org leaders are demanding this is the way forward. So you send your 40+ hours at work prompting till you can get a crappy MR out and hav it be ok to pass agent code review or have it fixed and push by the agents. The cracks are showing. All this large software is breaking and people do not care about the work anymore. The sad part is the ones who do care get pounded by leadership to give in and keep pressing the "enter" key into their favorite agent.

This leads to people pushing major breaking changes or death by 1000 cuts. This takes it toll on systems. The sad part is that when things break who do you go to? I mean you can't ask the programmer how their program works in the year 2026 that would be too crazy of an ask. So now we have to prompt again to understand what broke and why and sometimes its still not the correct reason.

Side note why does every company want all these new features? Spotify doesn't need new features they need to fix their current issues and optimize for better streaming. Why does DoorDash need a food ordering agent? This is so stupid it makes my IQ melt.

This blood ritual and obsession with productivity is mislead. There is a better way

## The Light in the Jack-O-Latern

I realize I can't control the direction of large companies but forcing more MRs that many feel lead to more bugs and useless features is fitting the square in the round.

There is a productivity boost that we can take advantage of and it may turn out to be more healthy in the long run. Agents allow us to work and explore a feature in more depth in a shorter time span. Instead of rushing out garbage you can use agents to think and design better systems. Even if you are more anti-AI lets at least use them to help search for certain patterns or docs for libs. Instead of rushing to a zero sum system that explodes craft yourself the best features possible.

You need to slow down and speed up at the same time. Do not rush the feature but explore how to make the feature better or if a research task then allow more a more thoughtful writeup or proof of concept. Don't get stuck on the small things (syntax, searching hours for docs, spending hours or days refactoring a small number of files). Use agents to help refactor into a few different patterns to see how it may be and then pick the one that is the best using your human intuition and craftiness. Only you understand the team, full system, goals, and customer.

You need to push back and say NO to your managers if in a position where you wont get sacked immediately. Spend your time choosing the best ideas to focus on and go deeper into making those algorithms, models, frontend, backend, much more efficient and deliver a supreme product.

I understand this may only be possible in a high trust small/medium size teams. I don't know how to fix large companies and teams that have signed up for this spooky pact that requires the burnout of engineers and terrible systems. Please Epic I beg you to fix the many half broken features in Unreal Engine.

## Conclusion: Fight Back Against Shallow Productivity Demons

These people obsessed with "productivity" are steering the ship into an iceberg. Instead help steer us towards greatness. We have so many tools to use these days. Care about the work you do. Care about the code and read it. Use and focus this productivity obsession of the managers into deeper and more meaningful features. You can push deeper than ever, be more creative, run parallel ideas, and create efficient software which we somehow lost along the way. Love and dig deeper into the craft.
